## ✨ Design & Features

- **Theme & Colors:** Deep royal navy blue background, champagne gold and ivory typography, baroque gold acanthus scrollwork with royal navy blue roses.
- **Opening Invitation Cover:**
  - Wax seal and luminous cream halo with maroon heart emblem.
  - Personalized guest plaque for **Evin Benny**.
  - Glowing champagne gold **Open** button that triggers a 3D curtain unroll transition.
- **Music Integration:**
  - Official audio from **"Sana - Aaro Nee"** (`https://youtu.be/il7oBI7lpNU`) starting **from 1:15**.
  - Floating vinyl player with animated soundwave equalizers and audio fallbacks.
- **CEREMONY INFO:** Dual-column parents hierarchy (*Mylakkattu House* & *Payyappilly House*) with announcement banner.
- **RECEPTION INFO & Calendar:** Auspicious date callout and interactive **January 2027 Calendar Grid** highlighting **`17 ♥`**.
- **Location & Maps:** Google Maps direction links for St. Sebastian's Church and Parish Hall.
- **Calendar Sync:** One-click **Add to Google Calendar** and **Download Apple / iCal (.ics)** file.
- **Live Countdown Timer:** Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds to January 17, 2027, 11:30 AM IST.
- **Photo Gallery:** Editorial photo grid with category filters and full-screen lightbox modal.
- **Special Scripture Verse:** *"A cord of three strands is not quickly broken."* — Ecclesiastes 4:12 with love from Mila Irene, Susmi & Salas.
- **Guestbook with AI Magic Wand (`🪄`):** Interactive wishes wall with auto-generated blessings and `localStorage` persistence.
- **RSVP Modal:** Attendance confirmation with guest count selector.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or start a local server:

```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 5173
```
Visit `http://localhost:5173/` in your browser.
